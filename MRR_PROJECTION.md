## What Drives Each Quarter

### Q1 Aug–Oct 2026 — Traction Phase
**Goal: First $1K MRR**

The hardest phase. No SEO traction yet. No word of mouth. Everything is manual outreach.

**What to do:**
- DM 10 CA firms per week on LinkedIn directly ("I built a tool that generates all ITR filing letters from Tally data in 20 minutes — can I show you a 10-minute demo?")
- Post on r/india, r/CharteredAccountant with a free tool angle
- **ITR filing season (July-Sept) is your cheat code** — CAs are drowning in repetitive document work RIGHT NOW. This is the single best time to sell to them.
- Launch on ProductHunt in Month 2 or 3 — even a Mid-day #5 = 300–500 new signups
- Demo video: screen record uploading an ITR PDF → asking "what is this person's total tax liability?" → answer in 5 sec. Post on LinkedIn + Twitter.

**Realistic conversion from demos:**
- 10 demos/week × 4 weeks = 40 demos
- 20% close rate = 8 CA firms/month × $99 Business = $800 just from 2 months of CA outreach
- This alone gets you to M2–M3 numbers

**Key unlock:** The first CA firm that loves it will tell 3 other CAs in their WhatsApp group. CA community is extremely tight-knit — one reference sells 10 accounts.

---

### Q2 Nov 2027–Jan 2027 — Compounding Phase
**Goal: $2K → $6K MRR**

By now you have:
- Real testimonials ("saves me 3 hours a day")
- SEO starting to show up (3-6 month lag from publishing)
- Referral loop starting (1 CA firm → 3 more CAs)

**What to do:**
- Bulk generation goes live (Phase 3) — this is the demo that sells itself. "Watch me generate 200 offer letters in 90 seconds." Post this.
- HR mode features → approach staffing agencies and manufacturing HR teams
- First agency/reseller deal: find one digital agency that handles multiple clients' documents and offer them white-label at $299/mo — they charge their clients ₹5,000/month per client, 10 clients = they profit ₹50K/month using you
- Chrome extension if shipped → passive acquisition from people browsing PDFs

**Conversion lever:** One staffing agency with 50 active clients on retainer = $99 × 50 = $4,950 recurring from a single reseller. You only need 6 of these to hit $30K MRR.

---

### Q3 Feb–Apr 2027 — Scaling Phase
**Goal: $6K → $16K MRR**

- SEO compounding: "chat with PDF", "edit PDF online free", "generate offer letter from Excel" — these are high-intent, low-competition searches
- CA vertical deep: ITR season returns (Jan-March advance tax). GST quarterly filings. Each filing cycle = CA firms feel the pain again = upgrade cycle
- Schema extraction live (Phase 7) → PE firms, insurance companies, banks can now extract data from 1,000 statements at once → enterprise-adjacent deals ($299–$999/mo)
- API platform → developers build on top → DocuQuery becomes infrastructure, not just a tool

**The agency flywheel by now:**
1 agency reseller brings 20 clients → 20 × $99 = $1,980/mo from one channel partner  
6 agency resellers = $11,880/mo just from resellers  
Add direct users → $16K total is very reachable  

---

### Q4 May–Jul 2027 — $37K MRR
**Goal: Predictable, repeatable growth**

- Monthly growth becomes more predictable (~30-35%)
- Churn stabilizes at 3–4% as product matures
- Annual plans coming in (2 months free → locks in users for 12 months → smooths cash flow)
- Team / Business plans dominate the mix (higher ARPU pulls the average up)

## Path to $100K MRR

| Scenario | When you hit $100K MRR | What gets you there |
|---|---|---|
| Conservative | Month 24–30 (Aug 2028–Feb 2029) | Slow organic, no viral moments |
| Base case | Month 18–20 (Feb–Apr 2028) | Consistent execution, 2-3 agency resellers, SEO compounding |
| Optimistic | Month 12–14 (Jul–Sep 2027) | One viral moment + 5+ agency resellers + strong CA vertical |

**Honest take:** $100K MRR in 12 months from zero, organically, is top-decile for bootstrapped SaaS. It's achievable but needs at least one of: viral distribution moment, a reseller/channel partner that compounds, or significant content/SEO volume early. Plan for 18 months, aim for 12.

## The Single Most Important Lever

**Agency resellers, not individual users.**

One reseller at $299/month who brings 10–20 client accounts is worth 100 individual free-tier signups. If you close 1 agency reseller per week from Month 4 onward:

| Month | Agency Resellers | MRR from resellers alone |
|---|---|---|
| M4 | 2 | $598 |
| M6 | 6 | $1,794 |
| M8 | 12 | $3,588 |
| M10 | 20 | $5,980 |
| M12 | 30 | $8,970 |

30 agency resellers × $299 = $8,970/month from one channel. Add in their client accounts that upgrade to Business plan independently = another $5–15K. Agencies are the growth engine.

**How to close agency resellers:** Find digital marketing agencies or CA "network firms" (one CA running a franchise of 10 associate firms). Offer: first month free, your branding on all generated PDFs, rebrand option at $299/mo. They see ROI on client #2.
